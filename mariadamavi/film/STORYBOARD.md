# MaviBag line: storyboard

## PAGE
- **Problem:** buying a sealed Apple product from a reseller feels like a gamble; a box in a mailer says nothing about who packed it.
- **Audience:** Brazilians buying a MacBook / iPhone / iPad / Watch / AirPods from Instagram resellers, scrolling the landing page.
- **Goal:** the hero makes "packed by hand, one at a time" visible before a word is read, then hands the scroll to the next section.
- **Emotion:** calm trust with a little delight (the pink peanuts are a joke you can see).

## Inventory (from the brief and the real MaviBag photo)
| item | value |
|---|---|
| logo shape | "MARIADAMAVI" set huge, condensed, vertical, in hot pink: the print on the bag IS the mark |
| core object | the MaviBag: frosted zip bag, dark tray, pink foam peanuts, black "CHEGOU." card with the 3% coupon |
| material | milky frosted plastic, matte pink foam, white sealed box, dark anodized metal |
| key verb | fill / pack (by hand, one at a time) |
| success moment | the bag swollen full and round, pink light glowing through it, card pressed to the front |
| voice | warm, direct, a bit playful (the page carries it; the film is silent) |
| colour logic | ground #0A0208 / #14060F wine-black; pink #E8197F is LIGHT and the peanuts, never a flat background; #FFB8DA for hot highlights |
| type | condensed heavy caps (Anton, OFL) only on the printed bag and the card |
| never | a flat pink field, page chrome, a logo end card, Apple's logo or trademark text |

## The idea, and the alternatives considered
1. **Chosen (from the brief): the MaviBag production line.** A jewel-like line turns one sealed box into one finished MaviBag; the hero is the bag filling with pink peanuts. Cover the logo and it still only works for this brand: nobody else ships Apple products in a frosted bag of pink peanuts.
2. Held shot: one hand-held bag on a turntable, peanuts settling in macro. Beautiful, but nothing for scroll beats to sync to.
3. Real-output montage: a wall of 500 finished bags. Proof by quantity, but loses the "one at a time" truth.
The brief already chose 1, and it is the only one that gives each page sentence its own physical action.

## Film system
- One clock (smooth, 30 fps), one continuous camera (dolly + slow orbit), no cuts, no fades, crisp (no shutter blur: it is scrubbed).
- Light: low-key; one soft warm-white key from the upper left; a pink rim from back right; pink glow from the floor slot under the bag and, during the fill, from inside the bag. Haze: exponential wine fog. Film grain at encode.
- Accent meaning: **pink = the care** (the peanuts, the light inside the bag, the scan line, the print).
- Hero per frame: the bag (box during beat 2).

## World
A dark wine-black reflective plane. On it, left and receding into depth: a short roller conveyor with a pedestal tray at its end. Overhead: one slim anodized rail with a carriage and a telescoping soft arm. On the right, at the end of the rail: the bag station: a clamp bar holding a frosted MaviBag open over a slot in the floor that glows pink. A short angled chute from an upper-left hopper ends above the bag mouth. A slim card arm on the right. Two satin-black zip rollers ride on the clamp.

## Beats (300 frames, 10.0 s, 30 fps)
| # | frames | role | buys | what moves | handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-30 | hook | the viewer sees a beautiful, empty, waiting MaviBag: something will be packed | camera creeps in, bag breathes on its clamp, floor slot glows | first roller turns, box enters far back |
| 2 | 30-75 | proof | the product arrives sealed and untouched | box glides on rollers toward camera; the soft arm lifts it into the dark tray | arm lets go, scanner wakes |
| 3 | 75-105 | proof | someone checks every piece | pink scan line crosses the box; arm takes the tray, swings it upright and lowers it into the bag | tray lands, chute opens |
| 4 | 105-195 | **the point** | care is abundant and physical | pink peanuts pour from the chute, bounce, pile, bury the tray; the bag swells round; pink light rises inside | stream ends, card arm descends |
| 5 | 195-240 | proof | there is a personal note and a gift | card slides down inside the front of the bag and settles against the plastic | card arm leaves, rollers start |
| 6 | 240-270 | proof | it is closed and ready | two rollers run along the zip; mouth closes behind them; peanuts settle | clamp opens |
| 7 | 270-300 | cta | there is one for you next | bag drops through the floor slot out of frame; next empty bag swings in on the rail and settles | ends in motion (the swing) |

Importance: beat 4 is the point (30 % of the film by time, 1 of 7 beats).
Picture with the text covered: every beat is a physical action on one object; no beat needs the page copy to be understood.

## Gates planned
- G1: no UI; the only type is the in-world bag print and card (card readable around frame 230 when the camera is closest).
- G3: no end card; the last frame is a swinging bag (end hold < 0.6 s by design).
- G4: one hero per frame (the bag; the box while it travels).
- Upper-centre third: kept to dark rail and hopper silhouette; the bright events sit in the lower two thirds.
