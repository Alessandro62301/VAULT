## Platform

## Stack

Vite + vanilla JS + GSAP (ScrollTrigger). Static build deployed to Vercel.

## Users

- People who live on their Mac/iPhone: creators, designers, developers, video editors,
  health professionals and students (dentistry connects to Maria's own path). They want a
  specific Apple device, often a high-end configuration, at a better price than Brazilian
  retail, without the risk of a shady import.
- Buyers who are afraid of imported electronics and need to trust a real person first.
- Companies that need several Apple devices (B2B, with invoice).

Typical situation: they found @mariadamavi on Instagram or by referral, and they decide
whether to message on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

mariadamavi sells the Apple ecosystem (MacBook, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods,
accessories) sealed and imported from the USA, plus semi-new devices. The landing page
exists to turn trust into a WhatsApp conversation. Success = qualified WhatsApp messages.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before price: every section answers "can I trust these people?".
2. People, not a logo: Maria and Junior are visible; the store has a face.
3. Care is the product: the MaviBag and the process are the differentiator.
4. Every path ends in a WhatsApp conversation, with a pre-filled message saying where it came from.
5. Facts are stated plainly: warranty, deadlines, trade-in rules, no vague promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

for all scroll-driven and 3D effects. Brazilian Portuguese.
